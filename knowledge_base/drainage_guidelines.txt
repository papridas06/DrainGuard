DRAINAGE INSPECTION GUIDANCE

Purpose:
This knowledge base provides general guidance for drainage
inspection and maintenance decision support.

VISIBLE DEBRIS:
Visible leaves, plastic, mud or other debris around drainage
inlets should be physically inspected and cleared according
to local maintenance procedures.

STANDING WATER:
Standing water around a drainage inlet can indicate a need
for field verification of inlet clearance and downstream flow.

HEAVY RAINFALL:
During heavy rainfall, drainage assets in locations with
historical waterlogging may require increased inspection
attention.

HUMAN VERIFICATION:
AI-generated observations are indicators only. Physical
inspection should be performed before maintenance decisions.

LIMITATIONS:
Image-based screening cannot confirm underground blockage,
subsurface conditions, pipe integrity or future flood
occurrence.
